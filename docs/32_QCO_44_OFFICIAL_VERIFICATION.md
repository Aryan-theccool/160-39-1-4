## 1. OFFICIAL EVIDENCE VERIFICATION FRAMEWORK

Each of the 44 seed candidate standards in `SEED_QCO` was rigorously evaluated against official Government of India Gazette notifications, Ministry Quality Control Orders (QCOs), and the Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) Compulsory Certification registers under the **Bureau of Indian Standards Act, 2016**.

### Evidence Hierarchy
1. **Level 1 — Primary Legal / Government Evidence**:
   - Official Gazette / e-Gazette notifications (S.O. and G.S.R. numbers)
   - Notifications issued by Central Ministries (DPIIT, Ministry of Steel, MeitY, MoRTH, MoCAFPD, MoHFW/CDSCO, PESO)
2. **Level 2 — Official BIS Registers**:
   - BIS Products Under Compulsory Certification (Scheme I / Scheme II / Scheme IV / CRS)
   - Official BIS Product Manuals and Gazette transition notices
3. **Level 3 — Non-Authoritative Secondary Sources**:
   - Industry portals and legal summaries used *only* for discovery and never as sole justification for verification.

### Evidence State Definitions
* **`VERIFIED`**: Official Gazette notification / Ministry QCO legally establishes that the product and referenced Indian Standard are subject to mandatory certification.
* **`NOT_FOUND`**: No official central gazette notification or mandatory BIS certification order was found in active enforcement.
* **`NOT_APPLICABLE`**: The candidate standard is either a horizontal test method, a voluntary code of practice, or a seed catalog title mismatch not subject to a product QCO.
* **`AMBIGUOUS`**: QCO evidence exists for closely related standards/schedules, but scope demarcation or ductile/cast iron transition requires further gazette annexure confirmation.
* **`SUPERSEDED_OR_CHANGED`**: A statutory QCO previously covered the individual standard grade, but BIS has amalgamated or superseded the standard into a parent specification (e.g. `IS 8112` and `IS 12269` subsumed into `IS 269:2015`).
