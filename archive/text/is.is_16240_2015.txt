INDIAN STANDARD
IS 16240:2015
REVERSE OSMOSIS (RO) BASED POINT-OF-USE (POU) WATER TREATMENT SYSTEMS — SPECIFICATION
Bureau of Indian Standards, Manak Bhavan, New Delhi

Technical Committee: CHD 13 (Chemical Engineering)
Year of Publication: 2015
Status: Current Indian Standard
Source: https://archive.org/details/gov.in.is.16240.2015

1. SCOPE AND FIELD OF APPLICATION
1.1 This Indian Standard prescribes the requirements, classification, methods of sampling and tests for Reverse Osmosis (RO) based Point-of-Use (PoU) Water Treatment Systems — Specification.
1.2 The requirements of this standard apply to procurement specifications, tender evaluations, quality compliance checks, and statutory certifications.

2. SUMMARY & TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS
Specifies design, total dissolved solids (TDS) reduction ratio, recovery percentage, and microbiological safety for domestic drinking water RO purifiers.

3. MATERIAL, CHEMICAL AND PHYSICAL SPECIFICATIONS
3.1 All materials used in the manufacture shall conform to the applicable Indian Standards.
3.2 Physical and mechanical properties including tensile strength, yield stress, elongation, dimensional tolerances, pressure rating, and thermal performance shall strictly meet the specified test parameters.
3.3 Finished products shall be free from defects, cracks, seams, surface flaws, and structural voids.

4. SAMPLING, INSPECTION AND COMPLIANCE TESTING
4.1 Representative samples shall be drawn from each manufacturing lot according to statistical sampling procedures.
4.2 Acceptance tests and routine type tests shall be conducted to verify conformity with all performance criteria.
4.3 Test certificates shall be provided indicating chemical analysis and mechanical test values.

5. MARKING AND PACKING
5.1 Each item or packing unit shall be legibly and indelibly marked with: Manufacturer's name or trademark, Standard designation (IS 16240:2015), Grade/Type/Rating, Batch number, and Date of manufacture.
5.2 Products complying with the requirements may be marked with the Standard Mark (BIS Certification Mark).
