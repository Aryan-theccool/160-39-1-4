INDIAN STANDARD
IS 16221 (Part 2):2015
Safety of Power Converters for Use in Photovoltaic Power Systems, Part 2: Particular Requirements for Inverters

1. SCOPE AND FIELD OF APPLICATION
1.1 This standard specifies the technical requirements, material characteristics, performance parameters, testing procedures, and acceptance criteria for safety of power converters for use in photovoltaic power systems, part 2: particular requirements for inverters.
1.2 This standard applies to public procurement, municipal engineering, industrial infrastructure, building construction, and quality compliance audits across India.
1.3 All materials supplied under this standard shall conform to the specifications and quality benchmarks stipulated herein.

2. NORMATIVE REFERENCES
2.1 The following standards contain provisions which, through reference in this text, constitute provisions of this standard:
- IS 2062: Hot Rolled Medium and High Tensile Structural Steel
- IS 456: Plain and Reinforced Concrete - Code of Practice
- IS 3043: Code of Practice for Earthing
- IS 1367: Technical Supply Conditions for Threaded Steel Fasteners
- IS 1200: Method of Measurement of Building and Civil Engineering Works

3. TERMINOLOGY AND DEFINITIONS
3.1 For the purpose of this standard, the terms, definitions, and symbols specified in relevant Indian Standards shall apply.
3.2 Lot: A collection of units or products manufactured under homogeneous conditions and submitted for inspection at one time.
3.3 Conformance: Verification that every sample tested meets or exceeds the minimum specified limit values.

4. MATERIAL SPECIFICATIONS AND TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS
4.1 The materials used in manufacturing shall be sound, homogeneous, free from structural defects, cracks, laminations, surface blemishes, or detrimental impurities.
4.2 Chemical composition, physical dimensions, mechanical strength, and electrical insulation properties shall strictly adhere to prescribed tolerance limits.
4.3 Environmental durability, corrosion resistance, UV stability, and thermal endurance ratings shall satisfy long-term outdoor and indoor operating environments.

5. PERFORMANCE REQUIREMENTS AND QUALITY BENCHMARKS
5.1 High Reliability Benchmark: The product shall demonstrate reliable continuous operation under rated ambient temperatures (-10°C to +55°C) and relative humidity up to 95%.
5.2 Factor of Safety: Minimum design factor of safety shall be as mandated for critical public infrastructure applications.
5.3 Energy Efficiency and Environmental Compliance: The product design shall minimize energy consumption and environmental lifecycle impact.

6. SAMPLING AND ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA
6.1 Sampling Scheme: Representative samples shall be drawn at random from each submitted inspection lot in accordance with IS 2500 sampling tables.
6.2 Acceptance Limit: If all test specimens satisfy the mandatory tensile, dielectric, hydrostatic, or dimensional criteria, the entire lot shall be accepted.
6.3 Re-test Protocol: If any specimen fails on a single parameter, double the initial number of test specimens shall be drawn from the same lot and re-tested.

7. TESTING METHODS AND QUALITY CONTROL AUDIT
7.1 Routine Tests: Conducted by the manufacturer on 100% of finished products prior to dispatch.
7.2 Type Tests: Comprehensive laboratory qualification tests to verify design integrity under simulated maximum stress conditions.
7.3 Acceptance Tests: Witnessed testing performed at manufacturer premises by the purchaser's authorized inspecting engineer before final dispatch approval.

8. MARKING, PACKAGING AND STORAGE
8.1 Marking: Each standard unit or container shall be legibly and indelibly marked with:
    a) Manufacturer's name, brand name, and registered trade-mark;
    b) Standard designation (IS 16221 (Part 2):2015);
    c) Batch number, lot identification, and date of manufacture;
    d) Standard BIS Certification Mark (Standard Mark) where applicable.
8.2 Packaging: Units shall be securely packaged in weather-resistant crates, bags, or reels to prevent transit damage and moisture ingress.
